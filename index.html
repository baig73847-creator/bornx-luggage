BORN IN SOUTH BRONX, NEW YORK

FORGED FROM RELENTLESS INDUSTRIAL RESILIENCE

Bronx Luggage was founded in an old iron foundry in the South Bronx by former aerospace engineers and obsessive travelers. Frustrated by $1,400 heritage brands that dented on the first flight and $150 high-street bags whose zippers burst open on terminal carousels, we set out to build an unbreakable travel chassis.

We eliminated the zipper entirely. We replaced cheap molded plastic with aircraft-spec 6061-T6 aluminum and Bayer Makrolon®. Every single component is engineered to be infinitely repairable with modular screws—because true luxury is a product that outlives its owner.
